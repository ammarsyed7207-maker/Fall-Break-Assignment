Historical Setting & Revelation Context

Makki vs. Madani

Surahs revealed before the Hijrah (Makki) often focus on faith, creed, character, and perseverance during hardship. Surahs revealed after the Hijrah (Madani) commonly detail community laws, ethics, and civic duties.

Qur'anic Arabic Linguistics

Root Words & Semantic Depth

3 Core Terms

Qur'anic words carry rich depths that single-word English translations often miss. Choose 3 key Arabic terms from your Surah. Research their three-letter root (جذر) and analyze how the word’s root meaning expands its spiritual significance.

Arabic Word & 3-Letter Root Common English Translation Deeper Connotation & Literary Meaning
تقوى (Taqwa)
Root: و - ق - ي (W-Q-Y) 		"Fear of God" or "Piety" To build a protective shield around one's heart; being actively conscious of Allah in every choice so you avoid spiritual harm.
